Magnesium Bisglycinate
Bulk Powders · 2 tablets · ~300mg elemental Mg
Take 30–60 min before bed
Why you're taking it
—
Sleep. Magnesium regulates GABA — the brain chemical enabling deep sleep. Better sleep = lower cortisol = less water retention + better muscle repair.
—
Fibromyalgia pain. Magnesium regulates NMDA pain-signalling receptors. Most people with fibromyalgia are deficient. Supplementing directly reduces pain amplification.
—
Muscle function. Required for muscle contraction and relaxation at a cellular level. Prevents cramping and excessive soreness after sessions.
Label note: Bisglycinate is ~15% elemental Mg. 2 tablets = ~300mg elemental. That is the therapeutic dose.
Streak: 0 days
Creatine Monohydrate
Bulk Powders · 5g daily · anytime
Mix with water or food. Consistency over timing.
Why you're taking it
—
More reps = more muscle. Creatine increases phosphocreatine in muscle — the immediate fuel for lifting. More reps per set means greater training stimulus.
—
Bone density. Combined with resistance training, improves bone density markers — relevant given OA and the post-fracture ankle.
—
Cognitive benefits. The brain uses creatine too. Evidence shows memory improvements in older adults. An underrated bonus.
—
Scale warning — important. Expect 1–2kg increase weeks 1–2. This is water pulled into muscle cells — not fat. Do not panic. It's a sign it's working.
Streak: 0 days
